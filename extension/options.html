PDF AI Reader

Setup and settings

Companion host

PDF AI Reader needs its local macOS companion to edit Markdown files and run Codex or Claude. The companion requires Node.js 18 or newer.

Get the companion

After installation, reload PDF AI Reader and enable Allow access to file URLs on its Chrome or Arc extension-details page.

AI data sharing

Highlighting stays local. If you choose an AI explanation, your selected text and relevant project-file contents read by the chosen CLI may be sent to OpenAI or Anthropic under your account.

Highlight markers

These strings are inserted immediately before and after the matching Markdown text.

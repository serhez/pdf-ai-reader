PDF AI Reader

Privacy

Local highlights

Highlight actions send the selected text and document location to the PDF AI Reader companion on this computer. The companion edits the matching local Markdown file. The developer does not receive this data.

AI explanations

When you explicitly choose an explanation and accept the disclosure, the companion starts your selected Codex or Claude CLI. Your selection and relevant project-file contents read by that CLI may be sent to OpenAI or Anthropic under your authenticated account and provider settings.

Browser storage

Highlight markers and your disclosure choice are kept in local extension storage. The pending request and latest displayed result are kept in session-only extension storage. PDF AI Reader has no developer-operated server, analytics, or advertising.
